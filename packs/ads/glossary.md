# Metric glossary

Always use the full name on first mention, e.g. "Cost per Acquisition (CPA)".

- **Cost per Acquisition (CPA)** — spend / conversions. Lower is better.
- **Click-Through Rate (CTR)** — clicks / impressions. Higher means more engaging ads.
- **Cost per Click (CPC)** — spend / clicks. Lower is better.
- **Cost per Thousand Impressions (CPM)** — spend / impressions × 1000. Cost of reach.
- **Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)** — conversion_value / spend. Above 1.0 is profitable. Google only; other platforms report no revenue.
- **Spend** — amount paid to the platform in the period, USD.
- **Conversions** — qualified outcomes (signups, purchases).
- **Reach / Frequency** — unique users and impressions per user. Facebook only.
- **Quality Score** — Google's 1–10 relevance rating; higher lowers CPC.
- **Video completion** — video_watch_100 / video_views. TikTok only.
- **Engagement Rate** — (likes + shares + comments) / impressions.

Platform-specific columns are 0 on other platforms — never compare them across platforms.
